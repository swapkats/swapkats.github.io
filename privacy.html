Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Swapnil Singh ("I", "me", or "my") handles information in connection with my mobile applications ("Apps").

Information Collection

My Apps are designed with your privacy in mind. I do not collect, store, or share any personal information from users. The Apps do not require you to create an account or provide any personal details to use them.

Data Storage

Any data you create or input within the Apps is stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to any external servers or third parties. You have full control over your data and can delete it at any time by removing the App from your device.

Analytics

The Apps may use Apple's built-in analytics to collect anonymous usage data, such as app crashes and general usage patterns. This data is aggregated and does not identify individual users. You can opt out of sharing this data through your device's privacy settings.

Third-Party Services

The Apps do not integrate with third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or social media platforms that would collect your personal information.

Children's Privacy

My Apps do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the Apps are safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact
